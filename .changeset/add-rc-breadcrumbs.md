Adds `rc-breadcrumbs`, a breadcrumb trail enhancer for a native `<nav>` and ordered list of
links that follows the WAI-ARIA Breadcrumb pattern. It names an unlabeled landmark in the
element's language, draws separators that assistive technology skips, and moves
`aria-current="page"` to the link matching `current-href` after client-side navigation.
The aggregate, the React wrappers, and all four themes cover it.
